I am a PhD student at the [Institute of Neural Information Processing](https://www.uni-ulm.de/en/in/institute-of-neural-information-processing/) at Ulm University, supervised by [Prof. Dr. Dr. Daniel Alexander Braun](https://www.uni-ulm.de/in/neuroinformatik/institut/hidden/d-braun/). My research lies at the intersection of reinforcement learning, goal-conditioned learning, intrinsic motivation and world model learning. I am particularly interested in how autonomous agents can learn and adapt in complex, dynamic environments, driven by internal goals and curiosity. I am also interested in agent-based modeling and its applications in computational economics.

I hold an M.Sc. in Cognitive Systems from Ulm University, an interdisciplinary program combining computer science and psychology, and a B.Eng. in Electronic Science and Technology from Southeast University, China.
